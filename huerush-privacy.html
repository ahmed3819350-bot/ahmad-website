HueRush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 21 September 2026

HueRush does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your coins, pets, level and settings are saved on your device and never sent to us.

1. About HueRush

HueRush is a casual game for Android in which you steer a rolling ball through a toy-box world, matching its colour to the gates ahead. It is made by Ahmad Adil, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for this policy.

2. What stays on your device

To remember your progress between games, HueRush saves the following in your device's storage for the app:

  • Your coin balance
  • The pets you have unlocked, and which one you chose
  • The level you are on and the furthest level you have reached
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings

This information never leaves your device. None of it identifies you. Uninstalling HueRush, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

3. What we collect

Nothing. HueRush has no accounts or sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not need an internet connection to play, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration when you bump into something or your run ends. You can turn this off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet: the game engine includes this permission by default. HueRush does not currently use it to send or receive any data.

5. Third parties

HueRush is built with the Unity game engine. Unity's analytics services are switched off in HueRush, so the game does not send gameplay or device information to Unity Technologies.

You download HueRush through Google Play, which handles the download and updates under Google's privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your device, Google may pass us anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not identify you.

6. Children

HueRush collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. You control everything the game saves, and can remove it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. If you have a concern about how your data is handled, you can contact us, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of HueRush adds a feature that collects information, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the game's Data safety section on Google Play will change to match. The updated policy will appear at this address with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ahmad.adil.aak@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the HueRush Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.ahmadadil.huerush.
