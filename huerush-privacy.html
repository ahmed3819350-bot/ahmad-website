HueRush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 22 September 2026

HueRush itself does not collect or store any personal information about you. Your coins, pets, level and settings are saved on your device and never sent to us. Buying coins goes through Google Play, which handles the payment and shares some information with the shop code in the game, as section 6 explains.

1. About HueRush

HueRush is a casual game for Android in which you steer a rolling ball through a toy-box world, matching its colour to the gates ahead. It is made by Ahmad Adil, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for this policy.

2. What stays on your device

To remember your progress between games, HueRush saves the following in your device's storage for the app:

  • Your coin balance
  • The pets you have unlocked, and which one you chose
  • The level you are on and the furthest level you have reached
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings

This information never leaves your device. None of it identifies you. Uninstalling HueRush, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

3. What we collect

Nothing of our own. HueRush has no accounts or sign-in, and no analytics or advertising. You can play the whole game offline, and nothing about you or your device is sent to us.

The one exception is the coin shop, which has to talk to Google Play. Section 6 sets out what that involves.

4. Buying coins

Coins can be bought with real money in the shop. Every pet and every level can also be reached by playing, without paying.

Google Play takes the payment. We never see or hold your card details, and no payment information reaches us. Google keeps a record of your purchase, as it does for anything bought on Play.

To show each pack's price in your own currency, the game asks Google Play for it when it starts. That is handled by Unity's in-app purchasing code, described in section 6.

5. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration when you bump into something or your run ends. You can turn this off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet: used by the coin shop to reach Google Play for prices and purchases. The game itself plays offline.
  • Billing: lets the shop sell coins through Google Play.

6. Third parties

HueRush is built with the Unity game engine. Unity's analytics services are switched off in HueRush, so the game does not send gameplay or device information to Unity Technologies for analytics.

The shop uses Unity's in-app purchasing code to talk to Google Play. Unity states that this code collects your purchase history, device and personal identifiers, approximate location, crash logs and app diagnostics, and uses them to make purchases work and to measure its own service. Unity says this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with anyone else. Unity's privacy policy for players explains what it does with it. This applies from the moment the game starts, because that is when the shop asks Google Play for its prices.

You download HueRush through Google Play, which handles the download and updates under Google's privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your device, Google may pass us anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not identify you.

7. Children

HueRush does not ask your age and collects nothing about you itself. The shop's purchasing code collects what section 6 describes, for every player, and purchases are handled by Google Play under your Google account's own settings, including any family controls on it. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. For anything held by Google about a purchase, or by Unity through its purchasing code, contact them directly using the policies linked above. You control everything the game saves on your device, and can remove it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. If you have a concern about how your data is handled, you can contact us, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of HueRush adds a feature that collects information, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the game's Data safety section on Google Play will change to match. The updated policy will appear at this address with a new effective date.
